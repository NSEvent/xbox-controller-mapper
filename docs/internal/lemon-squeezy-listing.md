# ControllerKeys

Turn your controller into a Mac remote, shortcut pad and creative tool.

Map controller buttons to keyboard shortcuts, mouse movements, scrolling and
macros. Use different profiles for different apps, or add layers to put more
actions within reach. Review Anki cards, navigate from the couch, give a
presentation or control editing tools with the controller you already own.

- Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo, Steam Controller, 8BitDo and more.
- Touchpad and gyro controls on supported controllers.
- App-specific profiles, button combinations, macros and scripts.
- English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, German and Japanese interfaces.
- Native macOS app for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs; macOS 14.6 or later.

**$19.99 once. No subscription. Use it on all your Macs.**

Try every feature free for 14 days before buying. The trial requires no account
or payment card. Controller sold separately; available features depend on the model.
